# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [documentation](https://docs.laravelify.cloud), especially [Troubleshooting](https://docs.laravelify.cloud/troubleshooting).
- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/laravelify-cloud/laravelify/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open an [issue](https://github.com/laravelify-cloud/laravelify/issues/new/choose) with your version (`cat /opt/laravelify/VERSION`), the steps, and the logs. Remove passwords and tokens first.
- **Security problem:** do not open an issue; see the [security policy](https://github.com/laravelify-cloud/laravelify/security/policy).

Laravelify is maintained in free time and funded by [sponsors](https://laravelify.cloud/#sponsors).
